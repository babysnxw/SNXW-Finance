# Vision

SNXW Finance is a Personal Financial Planning platform built to help people make better financial decisions with clarity, confidence, and control.

The product is guided by these principles:

- Privacy First
- Security First
- User owns their data
- Financial goals are first-class citizens
- Planning over tracking
- Forecasting over guesswork
- Financial health over raw transaction volume

SNXW Finance does not store banking credentials. The platform is designed around user-controlled financial data, decision support, and long-term financial well-being.

The vision is to help users answer practical questions such as:

- Can I afford this?
- What happens if I pay more this month?
- Which goal should I prioritize next?
- Am I on track for my financial goals?

The long-term direction is a trustworthy financial companion that helps users plan, forecast, and improve their financial health without sacrificing privacy or ownership.
